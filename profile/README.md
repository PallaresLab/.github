# Pallares Lab

**Friedrich Miescher Laboratory · Max Planck Society · Tübingen, Germany**

The **Pallares Lab** studies the **evolutionary genomics of complex traits**, combining computational, comparative, and experimental approaches to understand how genetic variation shapes phenotypes.

🌐 **[Pallares Lab website](https://pallareslab.org)**
🧬 **[GitHub](https://github.com/PallaresLab)**


## 🔬 Research

Our work brings together genomic, genetic, and experimental approaches to study:

- Evolutionary genomics
- Complex trait genetics
- Genetic variation and phenotypic diversity
- Functional genomics
- Experimental evolution
- Comparative genomics
- Computational and statistical genomics



## 💻 Code & Data

This GitHub organization contains **research software, bioinformatics pipelines and analysis workflows** developed by members of the Pallares Lab.


👉 **[Explore all Pallares Lab repositories](https://github.com/PallaresLab?tab=repositories)**



## 🧪 Open Science

We aim to make our computational methods and research resources **accessible, reproducible, and reusable**.
