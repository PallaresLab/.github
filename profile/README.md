# Pallares Lab

**Evolutionary Genomics of Complex Traits**

**Friedrich Miescher Laboratory · Max Planck Society · Tübingen, Germany**

The **Pallares Lab** studies the **evolutionary genomics of complex traits**, combining computational, comparative, and experimental approaches to understand how genetic variation shapes phenotypes.

🌐 **[Pallares Lab website](https://pallareslab.org)**
🧬 **[GitHub](https://github.com/PallaresLab)**



## 🔬 Research

We're a research group studying how genetic variation shapes complex traits — and how that relationship changes across environments. Using *Drosophila melanogaster* as our main model system, we combine high-throughput genomics, population and quantitative genetics, and systems-level approaches to ask questions like:

- What genetic architecture underlies phenotypic robustness?
- How do genotype-by-environment interactions reshape trait variation?
- Why are some individuals more resilient to perturbation than others?
